WELCOME TO YOUR LAB

Curiosity is the only prerequisite.

Pick an experiment, grab a magnet, and see what happens. Every particle has its own power, defense, velocity, and appearance.

  • V select and drag. Shift-click adds to selection; Shift-drag draws a selection box. Double-click opens the inspector.
  • A adds particles. L connects two particles with a spring. E erases. H pans.
  • Scroll to zoom. Space pauses. Delete removes the selection.
  • Rewind restores recorded states (up to 20 seconds at normal speed). Pause and use the time slider to explore history.
  • Save downloads a portable JSON scene. Your latest world is also saved locally every few seconds.

The engine ports Daniel White’s proportional attraction and inverse-power repulsion, with modern integration, springs, collisions, and an editable interface. This is a new implementation, not an AMOS interpreter; the original Mincer module is preserved as reference.
